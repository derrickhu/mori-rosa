Second diamond: the same dirt covered with short dry straw tufts, brighter wheat-straw yellow.
Third diamond: a creek crossing the diamond, water the color of milky tea with a sky-blue sheen, dirt banks toward the left and right corners.
Fourth diamond: a flat scatter of broken red bricks and gray concrete on the diamond.
