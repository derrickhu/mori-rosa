A wide regional map concept for a phone apocalypse camp game, high three-quarter isometric view, one continuous outdoor ground plane. The cleared camp is only a small pad in the center of a much larger land. Thick dark-ink outlines, flat storybook colors, two-step cel shadows, cute simplified props, small big-head survivors, same drawing method as the reference camp picture.

Center, a modest warm pad of packed dirt the color of dry wheat straw and cracked sidewalk-gray concrete. On it: a small worn school-bus-yellow command bus with a roof dish, a wooden smokehouse with a kerosene-orange stove glow and a thin smoke wisp, galvanized water drums with a hand pump, two small big-head survivors (red tank top and faded yellow vest), and one empty fresh-lumber foundation with a hammer. Kerosene-lantern orange light covers only this small pad.

A narrow creek the color of milky tea crosses the lower half of the map, entering from the left trees, passing the camp edge, and leaving toward the right. Old-bamboo-colored reeds, flat stones, a simple plank weir, and a row of metal buckets mark a future water spot. The creek is a thin ribbon, the land around it stays dry dirt and ruin.

Upper left: a surviving forest block, dusty olive canopy and dark pine green, mixed with a few dead gray trunks. A wooden hunting stand, stacked logs, and a low bush with small tomato-red berries mark wood and food.

Lower left: a collapsed supermarket shell with spilled wooden crates, tin cans, and a tiny cabbage-green garden plot, a future food yard.

Upper right: a wrecked roadside gas station, rusted car hulks the color of old iron, and a junk pile of plastic crates and scrap, a future salvage yard.

Top edge: a collapsed highway overpass and a far band of leaning ash-gray apartment shells under a storm-cloud gray-blue sky.

Around the camp, several separate rubble parcels wait to be cleared into new buildable dirt: a brick pile with a fallen billboard, a half-buried truck cab, a tangle of dead vines and scrap, and two more empty straw-colored pads already half opened toward the forest and the creek. Dirt paths connect the camp to the creek, the forest, and the supermarket, showing where the camp will grow.

The camp pad is small. Forest, creek, supermarket, gas station, and rubble each have their own clear piece of land, so the map feels large and ready to expand. Cozy warm camp inside a decayed city. Pure illustration, one camera, one wide scene.
